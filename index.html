From observation to prototype

Make quiet easier to share.

The design starts with a real library challenge: reminders help for a moment, but activity can rise again when the room gets busy.

01 / Notice

Sense sound activity

A microphone module estimates the room’s sound level locally. The concept does not need to capture or store speech.

02 / Signal

Offer a gentle cue

A simple green, amber, or red light can help visitors notice when the shared space is getting louder.

03 / Learn

Review the pattern

A future dashboard could help staff spot busy periods and compare zones, after the hardware and calibration are validated.

Built around a human moment.

Designed for a library conversation, not surveillance. No voice recording is part of this concept.
